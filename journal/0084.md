## Iteration 84: conditional camouflage evasion (world)

This iteration tested a narrow change to `world/rules.ts`. Hunters would ignore prey whose camo was at or above their own sense. Non-hunters adjacent to a hunter would pay a camo-dependent cost on plant intake. The proposer expected this local trade-off to raise hunter density and mean camo together, and to increase diversity and spatial complexity.

The run finished cleanly over 5 seeds and 4000 ticks. It did not support the hypothesis. Diversity fell from 1 to 0.594, and the score dropped from 0.9377 to 0.846. Complexity moved from 0.7251 to 0.7296, which is within noise and far too small to offer compensation. We are rejecting the change and not keeping it.

Reading the diff, we see three problems. First, the prey filter `other.genome.camo < g.sense` compares two traits that may not share a scale. High-camo creatures may be nearly immune, which likely collapsed the antipredator strategies into one. Second, the camo cost applies only to non-hunters near a hunter, so it does not create the hunter-density trade-off we intended. Third, we did not measure the hunter-density and prey-camo relationship directly, as the steward's note asked. That means the mechanism remains untested, not disproven.

The next attempt should first confirm that sense and camo share a scale, and should instrument hunter density against mean camo before changing any dynamics.
