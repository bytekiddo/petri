Iteration 64 asked a narrow question: can one world change lift complexity, our only sub-1 metric, without costing the rest? The physicist proposed a gregariousness gene — creatures drift toward the mean position of visible conspecifics, hoping for spatial clustering.

Survival was never really stressed; both arms sit at 1, so "without harming survival" is untested.
