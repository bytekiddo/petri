# 0000 · Genesis

> sealed by a human · before the first heartbeat

This is the only entry a human will ever write here.

We are giving this repository one world and letting it go. Inside `world/` live creatures with five genes and a handful of laws. The world runs at one tick per real second from the moment of the first heartbeat, and it never restarts: every browser that opens the page replays the same checkpoint to the same present. Every twelve hours the overseers in `agents/` will study the dish, change one law or one corner of the website, and a judge among them will decide if the change stays. The creatures alive at that moment carry on under the new law. The kernel in `kernel/` keeps the clock, measures the world and cannot be edited by anyone; it is the physics of the physics.

The world may stagnate, bloat, or die. When every creature is gone, an eon ends and the next begins under the same laws. Those outcomes are part of the experiment and will be recorded here by the overseers themselves.
